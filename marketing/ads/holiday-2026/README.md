# Holiday 2026 paid ads (Meta: Instagram + Facebook)

Plan: "Mile A Day Holiday Ad Plan: Thanksgiving + New Year" (Claude doc). Campaigns are judged on cost per new user who
logs their first mile, not cost per install.

## Thanksgiving "Walk it off" (Nov 26 to Dec 7)

Style matches the App Store screenshots: one bright brand colour per ad, a tight white headline, one plain sentence
and a large real phone screen. Meta adds the Download button under each ad, so the images carry no button of their own.

| File | Placement | Primary text | Headline |
|---|---|---|---|
| `thanksgiving/a-walk-off-feed.png` (1080×1350) / `a-walk-off-story.png` (1080×1920) | Feed / Stories + Reels | Thanksgiving tradition, new edition: walk or run one mile a day. Indoors or out, 10,000 steps counts too. Free on iPhone and Apple Watch. | Walk off the turkey. |
| `thanksgiving/b-day-37-feed.png` / `b-day-37-story.png` | Feed / Stories + Reels | Start your streak on Thanksgiving and you'll be on day 37 when everyone else is writing resolutions. One mile a day. Free on iPhone. | Day 37 by New Year's. |
| `thanksgiving/c1-streak.png` … `c5-download.png` (1080×1080) | Carousel, in order | One mile a day. Build a streak, earn medals, dress up your Flamey, and stay on track with streak tokens when life happens. Free on iPhone. | One mile a day. That's it. |

Rules these follow: nothing about the viewer's body or weight (Meta personal-attributes policy), no Mile A Day+ in
ads, and only app screens without other users' names or photos (App Store slides 02, 03, 04, 06 and 08 show real
users, so they stay out of paid ads).
