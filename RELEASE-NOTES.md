# App Store release notes — Summer 2026 Update

Paste the block below into App Store Connect ("What's New in This Version").

just under it, so trim before adding anything.

---

Streak Tokens
• Earn Double Down, Streak Save, and Streak Assist just by running — then spend them to protect your streak when life gets in the way.
• Go far enough past your goal to earn an Assist, and rescue a friend the day after their streak breaks.
• The Pure Flame: a gold flame on your profile when every day of your streak was earned on the day.
• Tokens live on your dashboard, profile, widget, and watch, with meters that tick up as you run.

A New Dashboard
• One card for everything that matters: your mile, your streak, your tokens — and a greeting that knows what day of your streak you're on.
• Choose your style: Modern for a calm, focused layout, or Fun for the animated flame buddy. Switch anytime in Dashboard settings.
• Your streak flame is a real fire now. It starts as a coal, shrinks as the day burns down, and erupts into a blaze the second you finish your mile.
• A new Streak Flame widget puts that same flame on your Home Screen, next to your streak stats. Tap it to jump straight into the tracker.

Moments
• Lock Screen countdown: when your streak is on the line in the evening, a live countdown to midnight appears on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island with a one-tap Start Mile.
• Streak reveal: the first time we read your history, watch your streak count up from zero as the flame ignites.
• Token stories: when a token saves a day — yours or a friend's — you get the story, not just the math.
• Your month, wrapped: when the calendar flips, see your miles, your best day, and a card worth sharing.

Feed & Friends
• Comments and replies on posts, plus @mentions in captions and comments that tap through to profiles.
• Collabs: share a run together as a co-post that lands on both profiles, with a new Tagged tab for posts you're in.
• Swipe between friends' stories with a smooth new story viewer.
• Hypes are unlimited — cheer your friends as much as you want.
• A rebuilt post composer: photo and stickers on one screen, caption and share on the next — and sticker styles show the real thing before you pick.
• Pending friend requests show up on your dashboard and app icon, and you can accept or decline right from the notification.
• The feed refreshes automatically when you come back to it, and loads faster than ever.

Tracking
• Group walks stay in sync: in-app tracking now filters GPS noise and cross-checks your step count, so friends walking the same route finish with the same miles.
• Treadmill and indoor runs are easier to find, and runs from your Apple Watch, treadmill, and other apps import from Apple Health automatically.
• Road to your next club: Insights maps your streak as a journey — milestones you've conquered, where you stand today, and days to the next one.

Camera & Photos
• Pinch to zoom in the camera, plus a new 0.5x ultra-wide lens option for capturing more of your run.
• When posting, you can now pick a photo you took during your walk or run — not just brand-new shots.
• Saving a snap to your camera roll now shows a "Saved" confirmation and won't create duplicates.
• Do an extra walk or run? You'll now get a fresh photo prompt for each one.

Maps
• Pinch to zoom route maps in workout details and on friend profiles.

Fixes & Reliability
• Fixed celebrations occasionally re-firing (or workouts briefly disappearing) after a cold app launch, and mile-complete celebrations now fire every time.
• Fixed "Photo waiting" showing for a previous day's snap, and mid-run photos are always matched to the correct day.
• Fixed "Couldn't prepare that photo" when posting HDR or wide-gamut images.
• Fixed stickers jittering while dragging them, and stickers no longer clip at the canvas edge.
• Fixed Today's Progress counting a midnight-crossing workout twice, and Current Streak Stats showing the wrong streak length.
• Smoother handling when an in-app workout can't save to Apple Health.

Run your mile. Keep your streak.
